Privacy note

Short and plain: what this website collects and what it does not.

Free tools

The GST reconciliation, bank statement summary, invoice summariser and repayment schedule tools read your files inside your own browser. Your files are not uploaded to us or stored anywhere by us.

Callback form

When you request a callback, your name, mobile number, optional email and message are saved in our Google Sheet and emailed to us, only so we can contact you. We do not sell or share them. Ask us at any time to delete them: weavesolution.connect@gmail.com.

Website statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are. You can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or an extension.

Software we build for clients

Client software keeps its data on the client’s own computer or in the client’s own Google account. We access it only when the client asks us for support.
