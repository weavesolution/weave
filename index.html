Our vision

Good software should not be something only large companies can afford.

Software companies build for MNCs and large corporates, because that is where the budgets are. Everyone else gets two options: a cheap template that solves nothing, or an enterprise licence built for a company ten times their size.

A shop with four counters, a clinic with two doctors, a firm with fifteen staff — each has a real process worth building for. That gap is the whole reason we exist.

So we work the way a smaller business needs us to. Priced in proportion, set up in weeks rather than quarters, and answered on a phone number rather than a ticket queue.
