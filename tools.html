Free tool

GST reconciliation

Compare GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B and GSTR-2A for one client, month by month, and see which figures and bills do not match.

  • Reads the PDF, JSON and Excel files downloaded from the GST portal
  • Outward supplies, ITC and tax payment compared head-wise
  • Bill-wise sales and purchases, 2B against 2A
  • Excel and PDF working papers

Loan repayment schedule

Enter the loan amount, rate, tenure and disbursement date and get the full schedule with the interest and principal split for every instalment.

  • Fixed EMI or fixed principal
  • Moratorium months and a rounded EMI the bank has fixed
  • Year-wise (financial year) summary
  • PDF and Excel

Bank statement summary

Drop bank statement PDFs of one or many accounts and get opening and closing balances, receipts and payments, and an Excel report.

  • Works with most Indian bank statement PDFs, including scanned ones
  • Checks every row against the running balance
  • Tags NEFT, RTGS, UPI, cheques, charges, interest and loan EMIs
  • Leaves out transfers between your own accounts

Invoice summariser

Upload tax invoice PDFs, or a ZIP of them, and get an item-wise summary and a formatted Excel file.

  • Many invoices at once
  • Item-wise quantities, rates and tax
  • Ties each invoice to its printed total
  • Excel download
