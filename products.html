JournalDesk — accounting software

Double-entry accounts for traders and multi-branch businesses

Vouchers, ledgers and final accounts the way an accountant expects, without the clutter of a full ERP. The owner checks the books on the phone, and staff can send entries from the phone that land in the desktop books.

Runs on: Windows PC, with an installable phone app. Phone sync through the client’s own Google Sheet.

Ask for a demo
  • Receipt, payment, journal and contra entries, with duplicate and recurring entries
  • Several businesses or branches in one set of books, separately or consolidated
  • Day book, ledger, trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, graphs and ratios
  • Alerts for FD interest, reminders and periodic entries
  • Phone app: position, ledgers, day book and reports, with PDF and Excel
  • Monthly licence per client, data stays on the client’s computer
